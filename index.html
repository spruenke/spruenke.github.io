Welcome!


Welcome to my website. I am Erin, a statistician specializing in nonparametric statistics and biostatistics, with particular expertise in rank-based methods and hypothesis testing.

My work spans methodological research, statistical planning and the analysis of preclinical and clinical data, as well as statistical consultation. In addition to applied work in preclinical and clinical trials, I am interested in the development of rigorous statistical methods for complex biomedical questions.

I also teach statistics in a range of programs at Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin, Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, and Freie Universität Berlin. This website provides an overview of my research, publications, teaching, and professional activities.
